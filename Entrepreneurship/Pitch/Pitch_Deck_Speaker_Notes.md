# Dalla3 Kershak - Pitch Deck Speaker Notes & Deep Dive

Since your Investor Pitch Deck is designed to be highly visual and minimal (which is the industry standard), you need a strong narrative to accompany it. 

This document contains the **exact script you should say out loud** while presenting each slide, along with deeper details to help you answer questions from investors or professors.

---

## Slide 1: Problem
**What to Say (Script):**
> "Hello everyone. If you’ve ever walked through our campus between 12:00 and 2:00 PM, you’ve seen the chaos. Students have exactly 15 minutes between lectures, but they are forced to spend 20 to 30 minutes just waiting in physical queues to buy food. Because of this, students are actively skipping meals or walking into their next lecture late. At the same time, vendors are losing money because their capacity is capped by the physical crowd, and handling cash slows everything down to a crawl. There is simply no smart, digital system organizing our campus."

**Investor Q&A Deep Dive:**
*   **Q:** *Why can't students just bring food from home?*
*   **A:** *Convenience. Our surveys show 80% of students rely on campus vendors for hot food and coffee. They have the purchasing power; the bottleneck is strictly the wait time.*

---

## Slide 2: Solution + Live Demo
**What to Say (Script):**
> "Enter Dalla3 Kershak—the digital food court. We allow students to order their food while they are still sitting in the lecture hall. They open the app, browse the menus, and schedule an exact pickup time. They pay instantly through our closed-loop digital wallet. Meanwhile, the vendor receives the order on a simple, fast tablet dashboard. When the lecture ends, the student walks directly past the massive line, picks up their hot food, and leaves. What used to take 25 minutes now takes 30 seconds."

**Investor Q&A Deep Dive:**
*   **Q:** *What happens if the food gets cold?*
*   **A:** *Our system uses scheduled preparation. The vendor's tablet only alerts them to start cooking 5 minutes before the scheduled pickup time.*

---

## Slide 3: Market Opportunity
**What to Say (Script):**
> "The market size here is incredibly dense. We aren't targeting a spread-out city; we are targeting thousands of students trapped in a single geographical area. At ECU alone, we have thousands of students who eat on campus almost every single day. Food is a daily, repeated habit. More importantly, this is a completely untapped niche. Once we perfect the model here at ECU, there are dozens of private universities in Egypt suffering from the exact same problem, allowing us to expand rapidly."

---

## Slide 4: Business Model
**What to Say (Script):**
> "Our business model is extremely lean and profitable because we have absolutely zero delivery logistics. We charge a flat, micro-commission of 3 EGP per completed order. It’s cheap enough that vendors won't push back, but highly lucrative at scale. In the future, we will unlock secondary revenue streams by charging vendors for promoted listings—allowing them to appear at the top of the app—and introducing subscription features for advanced vendor analytics."

**Investor Q&A Deep Dive:**
*   **Q:** *Why 3 EGP and not a percentage (like 15%)?*
*   **A:** *University food is already low-margin for vendors. A flat 3 EGP fee is perceived as a "transaction fee" rather than a revenue cut, making vendor acquisition much easier.*

---

## Slide 5: Go-To-Market Strategy
**What to Say (Script):**
> "To acquire users, we don't need expensive billboards. We know exactly where our users are. We will launch exclusively inside ECU to start. We are placing giant QR codes at every cafeteria. We are recruiting popular students to act as brand ambassadors, and we are running highly targeted, relatable TikTok campaigns showing the pain of waiting in line. We will also run physical activation booths on campus: 'Deposit 50 EGP into the app today, and we'll give you 55 EGP in credit.'"

---

## Slide 6: Competition & Differentiation
**What to Say (Script):**
> "You might be wondering: Why not just use Talabat or Elmenus? Because those apps are built for delivery, not campuses. Delivery drivers aren't allowed past university security gates, and an order takes 45 minutes. Dalla3 Kershak is hyper-localized. We are built specifically for campus behavior: pickup-only, zero delivery fees, and zero fleet logistics. We have the first-mover advantage in this highly specific niche."

---

## Slide 7: Financials
**What to Say (Script):**
> "Because we don't pay drivers, our operational costs are incredibly low. Based on our projections, capturing just a fraction of ECU's daily orders will generate ~35,000 EGP in pure commission revenue in Year 1. As we expand to three more campuses in Year 2, revenue scales to ~80,000 EGP, and ~100,000 EGP in Year 3. Because our primary costs are just server hosting and targeted marketing, we project reaching break-even within the first four months of our launch."

---

## Slide 8: Team
**What to Say (Script):**
> "The team behind this is the 'Masterpiece' group. We are tech-driven student founders. Because we are students ourselves, we deeply understand the user because we *are* the user. We have dedicated backend and frontend developers to build a robust architecture, UI/UX designers to ensure the app is frictionless, and operations leads to handle vendor onboarding. We have a lean, fast-execution mindset."

---

## Slide 9: The Ask
**What to Say (Script):**
> "To make this a reality, we are seeking an early-stage seed investment of 50,000 to 100,000 EGP. This funding will not go to high salaries; it will be injected directly into finalizing our software infrastructure to handle massive 12:00 PM traffic spikes, purchasing the initial tablets for our anchor vendors, and funding our on-ground marketing booths to aggressively acquire our first 1,000 users. This capital allows us to go live."

**Investor Q&A Deep Dive:**
*   **Funding Breakdown (If asked):**
    *   40%: App Development & Server Costs (AWS scaling).
    *   30%: Hardware (Providing cheap Android tablets to the first 5 vendors).
    *   30%: Marketing (On-campus booths, flyer printing, initial wallet promo credits).

---

## Slide 10: Vision / Impact
**What to Say (Script):**
> "Our vision is simple: we want to become the number one campus ordering system in the country. We want to save millions of collective student hours, allowing them to focus on studying and socializing rather than standing in a sweaty line. We want to digitize the entire university dining experience and build a smart campus ecosystem. Join us in making the physical queue a thing of the past. Thank you."
